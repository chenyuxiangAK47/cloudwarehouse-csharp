--- Part 开场 · 约 20 秒 · 展示：封面或首页 ---

ZH:
各位老师好。这是 App Demo。
我演示两套能跑的系统：CloudWarehouse 云仓结算，还有 PDA 无订单报工。
以真实界面为主，少讲理论。

EN:
Okay, hello everyone. This is the App Demo.
I show two working systems: CloudWarehouse freight billing, and PDA no-order reporting.
I use real screens. Less theory.

--- Part 云仓入口 · 约 20 秒 · 展示：导航 ---

ZH:
云仓管理端有站点、目的地、客户、价格规则、成本价导入、客户报价导入、运单导入、规则查询。
下面直接进入结算主路径。

EN:
CloudWarehouse admin has Sites, Destinations, Customers, Price Rules, Cost Import, Quote Import, Waybill Import, and Rule RAG.
Next I go straight to the main billing path.

--- Part 主路径说明 · 约 15 秒 · 展示：运单页 ---

ZH:
标准顺序是先入库成本价和客户报价，再上传运单做预览对比。
为控制时间，价表已预先导入。我直接演示运单预览和双轨对比。

EN:
Normal order is: import cost and quote first, then upload waybills for preview compare.
To save time, price tables are already imported. I demo waybill preview and dual-track compare now.

--- Part 运单预览 · 约 85 秒 · 展示：上传 Preview ---

ZH:
上传账单明细，点 Preview。
系统按发货日匹配历史成本和客户报价，用 Strategy 引擎分别算应付和应收，再和表内中转费对比。
请看摘要：成功笔数、差异笔数。
成功行说明机器结果和账单一致。告警或失败行常见是退件、拦截等特殊行，可以解释，不是黑盒。
这一步把 Excel 对账变成可重复、可截图的闭环。

EN:
Upload the bill sheet, then click Preview.
The system matches history cost and customer quote by ship date, calculates payable and receivable with the Strategy engine, then compares with sheet freight.
Please look at the summary: success count and difference count.
Success rows match the bill. Warning or fail rows are often return or hold cases. We can explain them. Not a black box.
This turns Excel check into a repeatable, screenshot-ready loop.

--- Part Rule RAG · 约 20 秒 · 展示：点示例（可整段删） ---

ZH:
管理员忘了规则可以点示例查阅。只读，不改账单金额。

EN:
If an admin forgets a rule, click a sample question. Read only. It does not change bill money.

--- Part 切 PDA · 约 10 秒 ---

ZH:
接下来切到产线：霍尼韦尔 PDA 无订单报工。
和云仓是前后场：现场记产量，仓库对运费。本 Demo 不展示两套系统的生产级 API 联调。

EN:
Next, the shop floor: Honeywell PDA no-order reporting.
Front and back: shop floor records output, warehouse checks freight. This demo does not show a production API link.

--- Part PDA 登录选机 · 约 25 秒 · 展示：真机 ---

ZH:
登录进入应用，选择产线、机群、机床。
场景是夜班或临时加工没有正式 MES 工单，仍要采集工序和产量。

EN:
Log in, then pick line, group, and machine.
Scene: night or rush work has no formal MES order, but we still capture process and output.

--- Part PDA 开工 · 约 45 秒 · 展示：真机扫码 ---

ZH:
进入开工页。
扫批号。枪像键盘输入。有 XK 竖线前缀会自动去掉。整框替换，避免连扫叠字。
图号工序可手输，或扫组合码，图号分号工序名。
提交开工成功。
数据经 API 写入独立库，形成可追查流水。

EN:
Open start-work page.
Scan the batch number. The gun types like a keyboard. XK pipe prefix is removed. Whole field replace stops sticky double scan.
Drawing and process can be typed, or scanned as drawing semicolon process name.
Submit start success.
Data goes through API into its own database as a traceable record.

--- Part PDA 报工 · 约 35 秒 · 展示：真机 ---

ZH:
报工页提交成功。
真机上能看到约束：报工机台必须和最近一次开工一致。挂起后续开换机后，要在新机上报工。
请看成功提示。无工单现场闭环跑通。

EN:
Report page submit success.
The device shows the rule: report machine must match the latest start machine. After pause and resume on another machine, report on that new machine.
Please see the success tip. The no-order loop works end to end.

--- Part 收束 · 约 15 秒 ---

ZH:
App Demo 小结：云仓完成可核对的运单双轨预览；PDA 完成无工单登录、选机、开工、报工。
两者都是能跑的 MVP。谢谢。

EN:
App Demo summary: CloudWarehouse shows dual-track waybill preview. PDA shows no-order login, machine select, start, and report.
Both are working MVPs. Thank you.
