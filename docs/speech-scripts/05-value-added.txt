--- Part 1 痛点 · 约 35 秒 · 展示：痛点 PPT ---

ZH:
仓库：运费靠 Excel，历史价说不清，对账难。
产线：没有正式工单时靠纸笔或口头，产量和工序难追查。
所以一边要做规则加引擎加对比；一边要做扫码加规则加可查记录。

EN:
Warehouse: freight on Excel, history price unclear, hard to match bills.
Shop floor: no formal order means paper or talk, hard to trace output and process.
So one side needs rules, engine, and compare. The other side needs scan, rules, and database records.

--- Part 3 云仓硬增值 · 约 55 秒 · 展示：运单预览 ---

ZH:
云仓这边：按发货日取历史成本价和客户报价，再做应收应付双轨，并和表内中转费对比。
用的是账单期间的价，不是永远拿今天最新价。
对得上能看出来；对不上也能顺着原因查，比如退件、拦截。

EN:
For CloudWarehouse: history cost and quote by ship date, then receivable versus payable dual track, then compare with the freight on the sheet.
We use the price for that bill period, not always today’s newest price.
You can see matches. For mismatches, you can check reasons, like return or hold cases.

--- Part 4 规则查询 · 约 25 秒 · 展示：Rule RAG ---

ZH:
还有内置规则 FAQ 查询，方便查说明。
它只辅助查阅，不算正式结算。正式算钱还是 FeeCalculationEngine。

EN:
There is also a built-in rule FAQ lookup.
It only helps people read the rules. It does not do formal billing. FeeCalculationEngine still calculates the money.

--- Part 5 PDA 增值 · 约 95 秒 · 展示：真机 + 20 ---

ZH:
产线这边是霍尼韦尔工业 PDA，做无订单报工。
夜班或临时加工没有正式工单时，以前靠纸笔口头。
现在闭环是：登录，选产线机群机床，扫批号开工，再报工。数据经 API 写入 PDA_NoOrder。
三点：
一，车间终端扫码。批号去 XK 前缀，整框替换，避免连扫叠字。
二，挂起续开可以换机，但报工必须对齐最近开工机台。
三，组合码减少手输；异常巡检看错栏、工时异常、超时未报工。有图就闪一下。

EN:
On the line side, we use Honeywell industrial PDA for no-order reporting.
When night or rush work has no formal order, people used paper or talk before.
Now the loop is: login, pick line and machine, scan batch to start, then report. Data goes through API into PDA_NoOrder.
Three points:
One, shop-floor scan on the device. Batch removes the XK prefix, and whole-field replace stops sticky text.
Two, pause and resume can change machine, but report must match the latest start machine.
Three, combo codes cut typing. Anomaly checks watch wrong field, bad hours, and late report. Flash a screenshot if you have one.

--- Part 6 收束 · 约 55 秒 · 展示：收束页或镜头 ---

ZH:
小结一下。
云仓这边，对账从猜 Excel，变成规则、引擎，再跟表内中转费对比。
PDA 这边，没有正式工单也能扫码开工报工，数据落库可查。
规则 FAQ 只是查说明用的词法检索，不算钱；真正结算还是 FeeCalculationEngine。
技术上我把力气花在 Strategy 加深和应收应付双轨上，不是去吹生成式 AI 计费。
工厂侧也认了这两块本期交付：尤其 PDA 已经在夜班现场用。
我不是说全厂所有系统都替换完了，而是这期能跑的东西他们接受了。
报告、演示视频、可跑系统和 CI，这些实习要求的交付也都齐了。谢谢。

EN:
Okay, short wrap-up.
On CloudWarehouse, bill matching moved from guessing Excel to rules, the fee engine, and compare with freight on the sheet.
On the PDA, workers can start and report with no formal order, and the data is saved and searchable.
The rule FAQ is only lexical lookup for reading rules. It does not calculate money. FeeCalculationEngine still does billing.
Technically I spent the effort on deeper Strategy and receivable-payable dual track — not on generative AI billing claims.
The factory side also accepted both deliveries for this term. Especially the PDA is already used on night shift.
I am not saying every factory system is replaced. I mean they accepted what works this term.
And the internship deliverables are complete: the report, the assessment videos, a runnable system, and CI. Thank you.
